History

🎮

Kahoot!

Game-Based Learning Web & Mobile Free & Freemium 300M+ users

About

A game-based learning platform used for creating, sharing, and playing learning games or trivia quizzes. Founded in 2012 in Norway by Johan Brand, Jamie Brooker, and Morten Versvik, Kahoot! is widely used in classrooms worldwide to make assessment and review activities more engaging and interactive.

A daily record of using Kahoot! — observations, reflections, and a short video for each session.

Day 1 — First Look at Kahoot!
▼
✦ Notes & Reflection
Today I explored Kahoot! for the first time as a potential ELT tool. The registration process was smooth — I created a free account on kahoot.com in under two minutes. The dashboard is clean and well-organized, with large icons for "Create," "Discover," and "Reports." I browsed the public library and found hundreds of ready-made English quizzes covering grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension, and even pronunciation. My first impression is that Kahoot! was clearly designed with classroom engagement in mind. The bright colours, upbeat music, and countdown timer immediately create a sense of urgency and fun. As a teacher, I can see this being a powerful warm-up or review tool. One limitation I noticed straight away: most questions are multiple-choice, which limits the depth of language practice possible. Nevertheless, as a first encounter, Kahoot! feels accessible, polished, and genuinely motivating.

Day 2 — Technology Behind Kahoot!
▼
✦ Notes & Reflection
On Day 2, I focused on understanding how Kahoot! works technically. The platform runs entirely in the browser — no installation needed for students. Teachers host a game on a large screen (or share a link), and students join using a 6-digit PIN on any device: phone, tablet, or laptop. This is a huge advantage in heterogeneous classrooms where students have different devices. I tested the mobile app on Android and found it just as responsive as the web version. Kahoot! uses real-time synchronization, meaning all players answer simultaneously and results update live on the host screen. I also discovered the "Teach" mode, where questions can be assigned as homework, and the "Challenge" mode for asynchronous play — both are very useful for blended learning contexts. The platform's infrastructure is clearly robust, handling simultaneous responses from large groups without lag.

Day 3 — Exploring Quiz Types in Kahoot!
▼
✦ Notes & Reflection
Today I explored the different question formats available in Kahoot!. Beyond the standard multiple-choice quiz, I discovered: True/False questions (great for testing grammar rules quickly), Puzzle questions (where learners sequence items — useful for ordering events in a reading text), Poll questions (no right/wrong answer — good for discussion starters), and Type Answer questions (where learners type a word or short phrase, which tests spelling and recall rather than recognition). I created a sample ELT kahoot covering tense identification, vocabulary matching, and a short reading comprehension. Building it took about 25 minutes for 15 questions. The image and video embedding feature is a standout — I can add visual context to questions, which aligns strongly with multimodal learning principles. The variety of question types gives teachers more flexibility than I initially expected, though the platform still leans heavily toward discrete-item testing rather than productive skills.

Day 4 — Mid-Week Check-In

✦ Notes & Reflection
Halfway through the week, I paused to reflect on the overall experience so far. Kahoot! has exceeded my expectations in terms of ease of use and visual appeal. Day 5 — Gamification & Motivation in Kahoot!
▼
✦ Notes & Reflection
Today I focused specifically on Kahoot!'s gamification mechanics and how they interact with learner motivation. Kahoot! employs several key gamification elements: points, leaderboards, streaks, time pressure, music, visual rewards, and a competitive social dynamic. From a motivational psychology perspective, this connects strongly to Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan, 1985) — particularly extrinsic motivation through competition and reward. I tested the "Team Mode," where students work in small groups rather than individually, which introduces collaboration into the competitive framework. This is a much better fit for communicative language teaching, as it encourages discussion and negotiation of meaning before selecting an answer. I also explored Kahoot!'s "Koots" — short video-based lessons integrated into quiz flows. This blended approach between micro-instruction and formative assessment is genuinely innovative and aligns with flipped classroom methodology.

Day 6 — Strengths & Weaknesses
▼
✦ Notes & Reflection
Strengths: Kahoot! is extremely easy to set up and deploy — even technologically hesitant teachers can use it within minutes. The public library of pre-made kahoots is a significant time-saver. The immediate, visual feedback after each question helps learners see whether they were correct without delay. The platform works across devices and requires no student account for live play, which removes a common barrier in school settings. The results dashboard provides clear data on class performance and question-level analytics, which is useful for formative assessment.

Weaknesses: The competitive structure may demotivate lower-performing learners. The time pressure reduces the platform's suitability for complex reasoning tasks. Free accounts are limited to 10 questions per kahoot and 10 participants in assignment mode. The platform focuses almost exclusively on receptive skills (reading, listening) — it does not support speaking or writing tasks in any meaningful way. Additionally, internet connectivity is a prerequisite, which is a challenge in under-resourced schools.

Day 7 — Consistency & Progress Review
▼
✦ Notes & Reflection
On Day 7, I returned to kahoots I had played on Days 1 and 3 and replayed them to observe my own progress and re-evaluate the platform with fresh eyes. I notice that the repetitive format — same colour buttons, same music, same structure — starts to feel predictable after a week. For students using this daily, novelty will wear off, and with it, some of the intrinsic motivation. This is a known limitation of gamified tools: the "honeymoon effect" where initial excitement gives way to routine. I also reviewed the analytics from all sessions: question-by-question accuracy data clearly identified which vocabulary items or grammar points were still weak. This diagnostic function is one of Kahoot!'s most underappreciated strengths — it effectively turns every quiz into a needs analysis. I also explored the "study mode" feature, which turns a kahoot into a flashcard-style self-study tool, extending the platform's usefulness beyond the classroom.

Day 8 — Final Verdict & ELT Reflection
▼
✦ Notes & Reflection
After eight days with Kahoot!, my overall assessment is that it is a highly effective supplementary tool for ELT, particularly suited to vocabulary review, grammar consolidation, reading comprehension checks, and warm-up activities. It is not, and should not be positioned as, a complete teaching solution. Its greatest strength lies in its ability to rapidly increase classroom energy and provide instant formative feedback. From a constructivist perspective, Kahoot! supports knowledge retrieval and active recall, both of which are evidence-based learning strategies. The competitive element, when managed well (e.g., through Team Mode or Ghost Mode), can foster a positive, low-anxiety learning atmosphere. The platform's analytics also give teachers actionable data to guide subsequent instruction. My recommendation is to use Kahoot! strategically: approximately 1–2 times per week, with sessions lasting no more than 15 minutes, as part of a broader, skills-balanced curriculum that also develops speaking and writing competencies.

Criterion 1
🎯 Relevance
✦ Evaluation

Kahoot! is highly relevant to ELT practice. The platform directly supports vocabulary building, grammar review, reading comprehension, and listening skills through its quiz-based format. Its pre-made public library contains thousands of English-language kahoots aligned with CEFR levels and common ELT topics such as tenses, prepositions, idioms, and academic word lists. Teachers can also create fully customised kahoots tailored to their specific syllabus, lesson objectives, or textbook units.

The platform's applicability spans all educational levels — from young learners to adult professionals — and multiple contexts including face-to-face, blended, and remote learning. Kahoot! functions well as a warm-up activity, a mid-lesson check, a formative assessment tool, or an end-of-unit review. Its flexibility and direct alignment with language learning goals justify a maximum score for this criterion.

★★★★
02
Criterion 2
💬 Feedback
✦ Evaluation

Kahoot! provides immediate, visual feedback after every question — players see instantly whether their answer was correct or incorrect, and the correct answer is revealed to the entire group on the host screen. This instant feedback loop is one of the platform's genuine pedagogical strengths, supporting the "testing effect" in learning psychology (Roediger & Butler, 2011).

However, the feedback is shallow: it confirms right/wrong without explaining why an answer is correct, providing no corrective grammar commentary or vocabulary explanation. There is no personalised feedback pathway — a student who consistently gets tense questions wrong receives no targeted support from the system itself. The teacher must manually interpret the results dashboard and provide follow-up instruction. For this reason, a score of 3 out of 4 reflects Kahoot!'s strong but ultimately surface-level feedback mechanism.

★★★★
03
Criterion 3
🧠 Thinking Skills
✦ Evaluation

Kahoot! primarily targets the lower levels of Bloom's Taxonomy: remembering and understanding. Most questions involve recognising the correct form of a word, identifying grammar patterns, or recalling vocabulary meanings — all of which are foundational but cognitively limited tasks. The time pressure further reduces the cognitive depth possible per question, favouring rapid recognition over reflective reasoning.

That said, a skilled teacher can design Kahoot! questions that reach higher-order thinking. Scenario-based questions, inference questions derived from short reading texts, or puzzle-type sequence questions can engage analysis and evaluation. The "Type Answer" format also demands active recall rather than passive recognition. Kahoot!'s potential for higher-order thinking is therefore present but requires deliberate pedagogical design rather than being a default feature of the platform.

★★★★
04
Criterion 4
⚙️ Ease of Use
✦ Evaluation

Kahoot! excels in usability. The teacher interface is intuitive: creating a quiz involves filling in questions, typing answer options, selecting the correct answer, and setting a timer — no technical skills are required. The drag-and-drop question builder is simple and responsive. For students, the participation experience is even simpler: enter a PIN, type a username, and press a coloured button. No account is required for live play, which eliminates a significant barrier in classroom settings.

The mobile app mirrors the web experience faithfully and performs well on mid-range Android devices. The platform is also available in multiple languages, supporting diverse classroom contexts. Onboarding for new teachers takes less than 10 minutes. Minor complaints include a somewhat cluttered dashboard when managing many kahoots, and occasional loading delays during peak hours. Overall, however, ease of use is one of Kahoot!'s strongest qualities, earning a perfect score.

★★★★
05
Criterion 5
🔥 Engagement
✦ Evaluation

Engagement is arguably Kahoot!'s defining feature. The combination of upbeat background music, countdown timers, colourful animated graphics, live leaderboards, and a competitive multiplayer format creates a high-energy learning environment that is difficult to replicate with traditional assessment tools. Research consistently shows that Kahoot! increases student motivation, participation rates, and classroom attention (Wang, 2015; Plump & LaRosa, 2017).

The Team Mode option adds a collaborative dimension that aligns with communicative language teaching values, encouraging students to discuss and agree on answers before responding. The Challenge Mode allows asynchronous engagement outside the classroom, extending the platform's reach beyond a single lesson. The platform sustains engagement particularly well across a single session, though novelty diminishes over repeated use. Nonetheless, Kahoot!'s engagement credentials are among the strongest in EdTech, justifying a maximum score.

★★★★
06
Criterion 6
🔗 Sharing
✦ Evaluation

Kahoot! offers excellent sharing capabilities. Any kahoot can be shared publicly to the platform's global library, shared via a direct link, or shared via a PIN code. Teachers can share kahoots directly with colleagues, embed them in LMS platforms (such as Google Classroom, Canvas, or Moodle via LTI integration), or assign them as homework challenges via link or app notification.

The public discovery library allows teachers to find, duplicate, and customise high-quality kahoots made by other educators worldwide — a significant time-saving feature. Results can be exported as spreadsheets and shared with parents or administrators. The integration with Google Classroom is particularly smooth, adding Kahoot! directly to the student workflow without leaving the LMS. This makes Kahoot! one of the most shareable and interoperable tools in the EdTech market, earning a full score.

★★★★
07
Criterion 7
🌐 Accessibility & Cost
✦ Evaluation

Kahoot!'s free tier is genuinely usable for basic classroom purposes — unlimited kahoots for live play with unlimited participants, access to the public library, and basic analytics. However, meaningful limitations exist: the free plan caps assignments at 10 participants and 10 questions, restricts access to advanced question types (polls, open-ended questions), and does not include detailed individual student reports or the ability to remove ads during student play.

The premium tiers (Kahoot! EDU Standard and Premium) unlock full functionality but come at a cost that is prohibitive for individual teachers in lower-income countries. The platform requires a reliable internet connection and a screen capable of displaying the host interface — conditions not always available in under-resourced schools. Additionally, while the student interface is simple, some learners with visual impairments or reading difficulties may find the time-pressured, visually busy format challenging. These access barriers prevent a maximum score, making 2 out of 4 a fair reflection of the accessibility and cost situation.

Conclusion for Application Rubric Evaluation

A final summary of Kahoot! as an ELT tool, based on 8 days of personal use.

✦ Final Conclusion
Kahoot!: A Powerful Engagement Tool with Clear Pedagogical Boundaries
After eight days of systematic exploration, Kahoot! earns a strong overall score of 24 out of 28 on the Application Rubric Evaluation. It is an outstanding tool for boosting classroom engagement, delivering instant formative feedback, and making vocabulary and grammar review genuinely enjoyable. Its ease of use, excellent sharing features, and cross-device compatibility make it accessible to teachers at virtually any technical skill level.

However, Kahoot! is not a complete ELT solution. Its feedback mechanism lacks depth, its question types privilege lower-order thinking, and its cost model limits full functionality for many educators globally. It is best understood as a supplementary tool — one that complements, rather than replaces, communicative, skills-balanced language teaching. Used thoughtfully and in moderation (1–2 sessions per week, 10–15 minutes each), Kahoot! can meaningfully enhance the language learning experience, particularly for motivation, review, and diagnostic assessment.
✔ Recommended as an ELT Supplementary Tool

Reflecting and Critical Questions on Kahoot!

Reflective and critical questions situating Kahoot! within the broader discourse of English Language Teaching (ELT) theory and practice.

a
How can Kahoot! be applied in the ELT classroom?

Kahoot! can be applied across multiple stages of an ELT lesson. As a warm-up activity, it activates prior knowledge by reviewing vocabulary or grammar from previous sessions. As a comprehension check, it can follow a reading or listening task to gauge understanding through targeted questions. As an end-of-unit review, it consolidates learning through retrieval practice. In blended learning contexts, the Challenge Mode can be assigned as pre-class preparation or homework reinforcement. Team Mode is particularly valuable for integrating Kahoot! into a communicative teaching approach, as it prompts students to discuss and negotiate answers in the target language before responding — mimicking authentic language use.

b
What is the impact of Kahoot! in ELT?

Research indicates that Kahoot! has a measurable positive impact on student motivation, attendance, and participation in ELT contexts (Bicen & Kocakoyun, 2018). The platform's game-based format reduces language anxiety, which is a well-documented barrier to EFL acquisition (Krashen, 1982). Students who might hesitate to answer publicly in a traditional format are more willing to engage when framed as a game. The immediate feedback feature also supports self-assessment and metacognitive awareness. However, the impact on actual language proficiency gains is modest when Kahoot! is used in isolation — it functions best as part of a broader instructional strategy that also develops productive skills.

c
What are the advantages of using Kahoot!?

The key advantages of Kahoot! in ELT are: (1) High engagement — the game format creates a positive emotional climate that supports language acquisition; (2) Instant formative feedback — both students and teachers receive real-time data on performance; (3) Ease of access — students need only a device and PIN, with no account required; (4) Flexibility — kahoots can be used live, asynchronously, or as self-study; (5) Inclusivity through Team Mode — collaborative play reduces competitive pressure and promotes peer learning; (6) Rich analytics — teachers can identify common errors at question level and adjust instruction accordingly; (7) Integration — seamless connection with Google Classroom and other LMS platforms.

d
What are the limitations and weaknesses of Kahoot!?

The key limitations are: (1) Cognitive depth — the timer and multiple-choice format discourage deep thinking and favour surface-level recall; (2) Skill imbalance — speaking and writing are entirely absent from the platform's native functionality; (3) Equity concerns — students with slower reading speeds, disabilities, or limited digital access are disadvantaged; (4) Anxiety risk — the competitive leaderboard can heighten anxiety in low-confidence learners; (5) Novelty decay — engagement tends to decrease after repeated use; (6) Cost barriers — full functionality requires a paid subscription; (7) Shallow feedback — correct/incorrect notifications do not explain errors or provide grammatical explanations.

e
Which English skill components does Kahoot! develop most effectively?

Kahoot! is most effective at developing vocabulary knowledge (recognition, meaning, collocation) and grammar awareness (identifying correct forms, understanding structural patterns). It also supports reading at the sentence and short-paragraph level through question stems and text-based question types. Listening skills can be integrated if the teacher uses audio or video clips as question prompts. The platform is least effective for speaking and writing — these productive skills require open-ended, process-oriented tasks that Kahoot!'s format does not support. For a balanced ELT programme, Kahoot! should be paired with tools that address speaking (e.g., Flipgrid, VoiceThread) and writing (e.g., Google Docs, Padlet).

Fungsi diagnostik ini adalah salah satu kekuatan Kahoot! yang paling sering diabaikan. Saya juga menjelajahi fitur "study mode" yang mengubah kahoot menjadi alat belajar mandiri gaya kartu flash.

Kriteria 1
🎯 Relevansi
✦ Evaluasi

Kahoot! sangat relevan untuk praktik ELT. Platform ini secara langsung mendukung pengembangan kosakata, ulangan tata bahasa, pemahaman membaca, dan keterampilan mendengarkan melalui format berbasis kuis. Perpustakaan publik berisi ribuan kahoot berbahasa Inggris yang selaras dengan level CEFR dan topik ELT umum seperti tenses, preposisi, idiom, dan daftar kata akademik. Guru juga dapat membuat kahoot yang sepenuhnya disesuaikan dengan silabus, tujuan pelajaran, atau unit buku teks tertentu.

Platform ini dapat diterapkan di semua jenjang pendidikan — dari peserta didik muda hingga profesional dewasa — dan berbagai konteks termasuk tatap muka, blended, dan pembelajaran jarak jauh. Kahoot! berfungsi baik sebagai kegiatan pemanasan, pemeriksaan di tengah pelajaran, alat penilaian formatif, atau ulangan akhir unit. Fleksibilitas dan keselarasannya yang langsung dengan tujuan pembelajaran bahasa membenarkan skor maksimum untuk kriteria ini.

★★★★

Kriteria 2
💬 Umpan Balik
✦ Evaluasi

Kahoot! memberikan umpan balik visual yang langsung setelah setiap pertanyaan — pemain langsung melihat apakah jawaban mereka benar atau salah, dan jawaban yang benar ditampilkan kepada seluruh kelompok di layar host. Loop umpan balik instan ini merupakan salah satu kekuatan pedagogis nyata platform ini, mendukung "efek pengujian" dalam psikologi belajar (Roediger & Butler, 2011).

Namun, umpan balik yang diberikan bersifat dangkal: hanya mengonfirmasi benar/salah tanpa menjelaskan mengapa suatu jawaban benar, tanpa komentar tata bahasa korektif atau penjelasan kosakata. Tidak ada jalur umpan balik yang dipersonalisasi — siswa yang terus-menerus salah pada pertanyaan tentang tenses tidak mendapat dukungan tertarget dari sistem itu sendiri. Guru harus secara manual menginterpretasikan dasbor hasil dan memberikan instruksi tindak lanjut. Oleh karena itu, skor 3 dari 4 mencerminkan mekanisme umpan balik Kahoot! yang kuat namun masih pada tingkat permukaan.

★★★★

Kriteria 3
🧠 Keterampilan Berpikir
✦ Evaluasi

Kahoot! terutama menargetkan level bawah Taksonomi Bloom: mengingat dan memahami. Sebagian besar pertanyaan melibatkan pengenalan bentuk kata yang benar, identifikasi pola tata bahasa, atau pemanggilan kembali makna kosakata — semua itu merupakan tugas yang mendasar namun terbatas secara kognitif. Tekanan waktu semakin mengurangi kedalaman kognitif yang mungkin dicapai per pertanyaan, lebih mengutamakan pengenalan cepat daripada penalaran reflektif.

Namun demikian, seorang guru yang terampil dapat merancang pertanyaan Kahoot! yang menjangkau pemikiran tingkat tinggi. Pertanyaan berbasis skenario, pertanyaan inferensi dari teks bacaan pendek, atau pertanyaan urutan tipe puzzle dapat melibatkan analisis dan evaluasi. Format "Type Answer" juga menuntut pemanggilan aktif daripada pengenalan pasif. Potensi Kahoot! untuk pemikiran tingkat tinggi memang ada, namun memerlukan desain pedagogis yang disengaja dan bukan merupakan fitur bawaan platform.

★★★★

Kriteria 4
⚙️ Kemudahan Penggunaan
✦ Evaluasi

Kahoot! unggul dalam hal kegunaan. Antarmuka guru intuitif: membuat kuis hanya perlu mengisi pertanyaan, mengetik pilihan jawaban, memilih jawaban yang benar, dan mengatur timer — tidak diperlukan keterampilan teknis. Pembuat pertanyaan dengan drag-and-drop sederhana dan responsif. Bagi siswa, pengalaman berpartisipasi bahkan lebih sederhana: masukkan PIN, ketik nama pengguna, dan tekan tombol berwarna. Tidak diperlukan akun untuk permainan langsung, yang menghilangkan hambatan signifikan di lingkungan sekolah.

Aplikasi mobile mencerminkan pengalaman web dengan setia dan bekerja baik di perangkat Android kelas menengah. Platform ini juga tersedia dalam berbagai bahasa, mendukung konteks kelas yang beragam. Orientasi untuk guru baru membutuhkan waktu kurang dari 10 menit. Keluhan minor termasuk dasbor yang agak ramai saat mengelola banyak kahoot, dan kadang keterlambatan loading pada jam sibuk. Namun secara keseluruhan, kemudahan penggunaan adalah salah satu kualitas terkuat Kahoot!, layak mendapat skor sempurna.

★★★★

🔥 Keterlibatan
✦ Evaluasi

Keterlibatan adalah fitur unggulan Kahoot!. Kombinasi musik latar yang energik, timer hitung mundur, grafik animasi berwarna, papan peringkat langsung, dan format multiplayer kompetitif menciptakan lingkungan belajar berenergi tinggi yang sulit ditandingi alat penilaian tradisional. Penelitian secara konsisten menunjukkan bahwa Kahoot! meningkatkan motivasi siswa, tingkat partisipasi, dan perhatian di kelas (Wang, 2015; Plump & LaRosa, 2017).

Opsi Team Mode menambah dimensi kolaboratif yang selaras dengan nilai-nilai pengajaran bahasa komunikatif, mendorong siswa untuk berdiskusi dan menyepakati jawaban sebelum merespons. Challenge Mode memungkinkan keterlibatan asinkron di luar kelas, memperluas jangkauan platform di luar satu sesi pelajaran. Platform ini mempertahankan keterlibatan dengan sangat baik terutama dalam satu sesi, meski kebaruan berkurang setelah penggunaan berulang. Tetap saja, kredensial keterlibatan Kahoot! termasuk yang terkuat di EdTech, membenarkan skor maksimum.

🔗 Berbagi
✦ Evaluasi

Kahoot! menawarkan kemampuan berbagi yang sangat baik. Setiap kahoot dapat dibagikan secara publik ke perpustakaan global platform, dibagikan melalui tautan langsung, atau dibagikan melalui kode PIN. Guru dapat berbagi kahoot langsung dengan rekan kerja, menanamkannya di platform LMS (seperti Google Classroom, Canvas, atau Moodle via integrasi LTI), atau menugaskannya sebagai tantangan PR via tautan atau notifikasi aplikasi.

Perpustakaan penemuan publik memungkinkan guru menemukan, menduplikasi, dan menyesuaikan kahoot berkualitas tinggi yang dibuat oleh pendidik lain di seluruh dunia — fitur penghematan waktu yang signifikan. Hasil dapat diekspor sebagai spreadsheet dan dibagikan kepada orang tua atau administrator. Integrasi dengan Google Classroom sangat mulus, menambahkan Kahoot! langsung ke alur kerja siswa tanpa meninggalkan LMS. Ini menjadikan Kahoot! salah satu alat yang paling mudah dibagikan dan interoperable di pasar EdTech, layak mendapat skor penuh.

🌐 Aksesibilitas & Biaya
✦ Evaluasi

Paket gratis Kahoot! benar-benar dapat digunakan untuk tujuan kelas dasar — kahoot tak terbatas untuk permainan langsung dengan peserta tak terbatas, akses ke perpustakaan publik, dan analitik dasar. Namun, keterbatasan yang berarti tetap ada: paket gratis membatasi penugasan hingga 10 peserta dan 10 pertanyaan, membatasi akses ke jenis pertanyaan lanjutan (polling, pertanyaan terbuka), dan tidak menyertakan laporan siswa individual terperinci atau kemampuan menghapus iklan selama permainan siswa.

Tingkat premium (Kahoot! EDU Standard dan Premium) membuka fungsionalitas penuh tetapi hadir dengan biaya yang memberatkan bagi guru individual di negara berpenghasilan rendah. Platform ini memerlukan koneksi internet yang andal dan layar yang mampu menampilkan antarmuka host — kondisi yang tidak selalu tersedia di sekolah dengan sumber daya terbatas. Selain itu, meski antarmuka siswa sederhana, beberapa peserta didik dengan gangguan penglihatan atau kesulitan membaca mungkin kesulitan dengan format berbatas waktu dan tampilan visual yang ramai. Hambatan akses ini mencegah skor maksimum, menjadikan 2 dari 4 sebagai cerminan yang adil dari situasi aksesibilitas dan biaya.

Pertanyaan Reflektif dan Kritis tentang Kahoot!

Pertanyaan reflektif dan kritis yang menempatkan Kahoot! dalam wacana yang lebih luas tentang teori dan praktik Pengajaran Bahasa Inggris (ELT).

a
Bagaimana Kahoot! dapat diterapkan dalam pembelajaran ELT?

Kahoot! dapat diterapkan di berbagai tahap pelajaran ELT. Sebagai kegiatan pemanasan, ia mengaktifkan pengetahuan awal dengan mengulas kosakata atau tata bahasa dari sesi sebelumnya. Sebagai pemeriksaan pemahaman, dapat mengikuti tugas membaca atau mendengarkan untuk mengukur pemahaman melalui pertanyaan yang ditargetkan. Sebagai ulangan akhir unit, ia mengkonsolidasikan pembelajaran melalui praktik pengambilan kembali. Dalam konteks blended learning, Challenge Mode dapat ditugaskan sebagai persiapan pra-kelas atau penguatan PR. Team Mode sangat berharga untuk mengintegrasikan Kahoot! ke dalam pendekatan pengajaran komunikatif, karena mendorong siswa berdiskusi dan bernegosiasi dalam bahasa target sebelum merespons.

b
Apa dampak Kahoot! dalam ELT?

Penelitian menunjukkan bahwa Kahoot! memiliki dampak positif yang terukur pada motivasi siswa, kehadiran, dan partisipasi dalam konteks ELT (Bicen & Kocakoyun, 2018). Format berbasis game platform ini mengurangi kecemasan berbahasa, yang merupakan hambatan yang terdokumentasi dengan baik dalam akuisisi EFL (Krashen, 1982). Siswa yang mungkin ragu menjawab secara publik dalam format tradisional lebih bersedia terlibat ketika dikemas sebagai permainan. Fitur umpan balik langsung juga mendukung penilaian diri dan kesadaran metakognitif. Namun, dampak terhadap perolehan kemahiran bahasa aktual relatif sederhana ketika Kahoot! digunakan secara terisolasi — ia berfungsi paling baik sebagai bagian dari strategi instruksional yang lebih luas.

c
Apa saja keunggulan menggunakan Kahoot!?

Keunggulan utama Kahoot! dalam ELT adalah: (1) Keterlibatan tinggi — format game menciptakan iklim emosional positif yang mendukung akuisisi bahasa; (2) Umpan balik formatif instan — siswa dan guru sama-sama menerima data kinerja real-time; (3) Kemudahan akses — siswa hanya memerlukan perangkat dan PIN, tanpa akun; (4) Fleksibilitas — kahoot dapat digunakan secara langsung, asinkron, atau sebagai belajar mandiri; (5) Inklusivitas melalui Team Mode — permainan kolaboratif mengurangi tekanan kompetitif dan mendorong pembelajaran sebaya; (6) Analitik yang kaya — guru dapat mengidentifikasi kesalahan umum per pertanyaan; (7) Integrasi — koneksi mulus dengan Google Classroom dan platform LMS lainnya.

d
Apa saja keterbatasan dan kelemahan Kahoot!?

Keterbatasan utama adalah: (1) Kedalaman kognitif — timer dan format pilihan ganda tidak mendorong pemikiran mendalam; (2) Ketidakseimbangan keterampilan — berbicara dan menulis sama sekali tidak ada dalam fungsionalitas asli platform; (3) Masalah kesetaraan — siswa dengan kecepatan membaca lebih lambat, disabilitas, atau akses digital terbatas dirugikan; (4) Risiko kecemasan — papan peringkat kompetitif dapat meningkatkan kecemasan peserta didik kurang percaya diri; (5) Kebaruan yang memudar — keterlibatan cenderung menurun setelah penggunaan berulang; (6) Hambatan biaya — fungsionalitas penuh memerlukan langganan berbayar; (7) Umpan balik dangkal — notifikasi benar/salah tidak menjelaskan kesalahan atau memberikan penjelasan tata bahasa.

e
Komponen keterampilan Bahasa Inggris mana yang paling efektif dikembangkan Kahoot!?

Kahoot! paling efektif dalam mengembangkan pengetahuan kosakata (pengenalan, makna, kolokasi) dan kesadaran tata bahasa (mengidentifikasi bentuk yang benar, memahami pola struktur). Platform ini juga mendukung membaca pada tingkat kalimat dan paragraf pendek. Keterampilan mendengarkan dapat diintegrasikan jika guru menggunakan klip audio atau video sebagai prompt pertanyaan. Platform ini paling kurang efektif untuk berbicara dan menulis — keterampilan produktif ini memerlukan tugas terbuka dan berorientasi proses yang tidak didukung oleh format Kahoot!. Untuk program ELT yang seimbang, Kahoot! sebaiknya dipasangkan dengan alat yang menangani berbicara (misalnya Flipgrid, VoiceThread) dan menulis (misalnya Google Docs, Padlet).
